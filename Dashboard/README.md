Sales Performance, Product & Regional Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive Sales Performance, Product, Regional, Customer and Operations Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The objective of the project was to transform raw sales transaction data into an interactive business intelligence solution that enables stakeholders to understand revenue performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, customer behaviour, order activity, shipping performance, and product returns.

## KPIs
Total revenue 2.30M 
Total profit $286.4K 
12.47% profit margin.
Unique Customers: 788
Total Quantity: 23K
Total Orders: 6K

## Key Insights

- The business generates approximately $2.30M.
- The business generates $286.4K profit
- The verall profit margin was 12.47%.
- Technology generated the highest revenue and profit among the major categories.
- Furniture generates relatively high revenue but comparatively low profit.
- The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
- Consumer was the largest revenue-generating customer segment.
- Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping method.
- The analysis recorded 296 returned items.

## Recommendations

1. Investigate the low profitability of the Furniture category.
2. Continue investing in high-performing Technology products.
3. Investigate opportunities to improve performance in lower-performing regions.
4. Develop retention strategies for high-value customers.
5. Monitor product returns to identify recurring issues.
6. Evaluate shipping performance and customer delivery expectations.
7. Monitor product-level profitability rather than revenue alone.
